For the past decade I've worked across software, ML, and computer vision — 3D perception research for autonomous vehicles, four years building in-store vision systems at Everseen, and now Vision AI and VLM systems at Axon.

Outside of that I write **[The AI Merge](https://theaimerge.com)**, a newsletter on production AI engineering that's grown to 10,000+ subscribers alongside an NVIDIA content partnership. I also co-built **[Kubrick](https://github.com/the-ai-merge/multimodal-agents-course)**, an open-source multimodal agent course, with Miguel Otero Pedrido.
